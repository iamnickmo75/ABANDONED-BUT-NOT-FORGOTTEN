# ABANDONED BUT NOT FORGOTTEN
### A Journey Through NASA's Space Legacy

## Project Overview

**Abandoned but Not Forgotten** is an interactive educational website exploring the spacecraft, rovers, landers, and scientific instruments that NASA has left behind during its exploration of the Moon, Mars, and deep space.

Every space mission leaves behind a story. Some equipment was deliberately left behind after completing its mission, while other machines eventually stopped functioning or lost communication with Earth. Although these objects may no longer be active, their contributions to science and space exploration remain important.

This project presents their stories through research, images, videos, interactive animations, and a timeline of significant space exploration events.

## Project Objectives

- Introduce visitors to NASA's historical and ongoing space exploration missions.
- Explain why scientific equipment was sent to the Moon, Mars, and beyond.
- Explore what happened to equipment after its missions ended.
- Highlight the scientific discoveries and knowledge gained from these missions.
- Present educational information through an interactive and visually engaging website.

## Main Sections

### 1. Home
The homepage introduces the project and its central theme. It includes a featured documentary section and an overview of the three main research areas.

### 2. Project
The main research section is divided into three areas:

**The Moon**
- Explores lunar missions and equipment left on the Moon.
- Examines scientific instruments and experiments used during lunar exploration.
- Explains the purpose and scientific contributions of the equipment.

**Mars**
- Explores robotic missions to the Red Planet.
- Features notable rovers, including Spirit, Opportunity, Curiosity, and Perseverance.
- Discusses the challenges that affect robotic equipment on the Martian surface.

**Deep Space**
- Explores the Voyager missions and their journeys beyond the outer planets.
- Includes a historical timeline of important Voyager milestones.
- Introduces the Golden Record and the significance of the Voyager spacecraft.

### 3. About Us
Provides information about the people behind the project and its purpose.

## Interactive Features

- **Interactive space journey:** A visual rocket journey connecting Earth, the Moon, and Mars.
- **Mission exploration:** Visitors can explore the Moon and Mars research sections after reaching the corresponding destinations in the interactive journey.
- **3D visualization:** A 3D solar system model provides a visual introduction to space exploration.
- **Documentary videos:** Embedded videos provide additional context and storytelling.
- **Research timeline:** The Deep Space section presents important events in the Voyager missions chronologically.
- **Navigation:** Visitors can move between the Home, Project, and About Us sections.

## Technologies Used

- **HTML5** – Website structure and content.
- **CSS3** – Layout, styling, animations, and responsive design.
- **JavaScript** – Interactive navigation, mission controls, and website behaviour.
- **SVG** – Vector-based space animation and visual elements.
- **Google Model Viewer** – Displaying the 3D solar system model.
- **YouTube Embed** – Displaying documentary videos directly within the website.

## How to Run the Project

1. Download or copy the complete project folder to your computer.
2. Make sure the main HTML file and all required assets are present.
3. Open `videos added.html` using a modern web browser such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox.
4. Navigate through the website using the navigation menu.
5. Visit the Project section to explore the Moon, Mars, and Deep Space research.

An internet connection is recommended because some resources, including embedded YouTube videos, the 3D model viewer library, and externally hosted images, may require online access.

## Educational Value

This project demonstrates that space exploration is not only about reaching distant destinations. It is also about the knowledge, discoveries, and scientific progress made possible by the equipment sent there.

The objects left behind represent important milestones in human curiosity and technological achievement. Even when a mission ends, the knowledge it provides can continue to benefit future generations.
